Increasing access to digital sovereignty

Taddesse builds technology to increase access to financial sovereignty and digital independence across the Caribbean and beyond. Each of our subsidiaries unlocks different aspects of the digital economy for more people.

Together, we're helping build a financial ecosystem that is truly open to everyone, regardless of geographic location or economic status.

Island Bitcoin LLC
Develops software solutions that enable businesses to seamlessly integrate Bitcoin payment and storage capabilities.
Island Bitcoin Jamaica Ltd.
Creates accessible retail solutions and rewards programs that introduce Bitcoin to new users throughout the Caribbean.
Brave Island Mining
Provides sustainable Bitcoin mining operations powered by renewable energy sources, helping secure the network.
Island Bitcoin Foundation
Delivers education and community initiatives to increase financial literacy and Bitcoin adoption in underserved areas.
